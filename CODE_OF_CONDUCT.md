# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences**
- **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- **Focusing on what is best for the community**
- **Showing empathy towards other community members**

### Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment, bullying, or discrimination**
- **Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks**
- **Public or private harassment**
- **Publishing others' private information, such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission**
- **Other unethical or unprofessional conduct**

## Our Responsibilities

### Project Maintainers

- **Clarify standards** of acceptable behavior
- **Create respectful environment** where all participants can contribute
- **Address issues** promptly and fairly
- **Acknowledge and thank contributors** for their work
- **Be open to community feedback** and suggestions

### Contributors

- **Be respectful** of different viewpoints and experiences
- **Use welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Gracefully accept constructive criticism**
- **Focus on what is best** for the community
- **Follow project guidelines** and code standards

## Reporting Guidelines

### If You Experience or Witness Unacceptable Behavior

1. **Contact the project team** privately at:
   - Email: contact@assetfetcher.com
   - GitHub: Create a private issue or message maintainers

2. **Report clearly** with:
   - Specific details of the behavior
   - Screenshots or logs if applicable
   - Context and timing

3. **Avoid public confrontations** - Report privately for resolution

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment** within 24 hours
2. **Investigation** within 3-5 business days
3. **Resolution** with appropriate actions:
   - Warning for minor issues
   - Temporary suspension for serious issues
   - Permanent ban for severe violations

## Enforcement

### Consequences

- **Warning** - For minor violations or first offenses
- **Temporary Suspension** - For repeated or serious issues
- **Permanent Ban** - For severe violations or illegal activities

### Appeals Process

1. **Submit appeal** to project maintainers within 7 days
2. **Review by different maintainers** than involved in original issue
3. **Decision within 7 days** of appeal submission

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces including but not limited to:

- **GitHub repository** (issues, discussions, pull requests)
- **Documentation and guides**
- **Community forums and discussions**
- **Project events and meetups**
- **Social media channels**
